## 1. Executive Summary

This matrix cross-references domain concepts discovered during the textile repository archaeology against the **Versa Canonical Domain Model (v1.3.0)**.

### Canonical Baseline Reference:
- **39 Canonical Entities** (DocTypes in `versa_core`, `versa_textile`, `versa_jobwork`, `versa_quality`, `versa_export`)

- **3 Business Rules:** AVL requirement, 3-way matching, mass loss tolerance
